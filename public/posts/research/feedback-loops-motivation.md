# Real-time feedback loops that motivate learners
Designing tutor-facing signals to keep momentum without overwhelming staff.

## Signals that matter
We prioritize momentum signals-stalls, streaks, and bounce risk-over vanity metrics.

## Nudges that land
Short, specific nudges with clear next steps outperform generic encouragement.
