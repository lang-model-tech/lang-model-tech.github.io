# Why E2-volve
E2-volve is our adaptive layer that blends AI planning with tutor oversight. It cuts the lag between when a learner struggles and when a coach can intervene.

## Real-time insights
We surface friction signals in real time: time-on-task anomalies, question-level miss patterns, and confidence dips. Tutors see the patterns and can respond with contextual nudges.

## Feedback loops that motivate
Learners get gentle, actionable prompts; tutors get a queue of students needing help. This closes the feedback loop without creating alert fatigue.

## What comes next
Upcoming drops include cohort heatmaps, auto-generated practice sets, and exportable reports for admins.
