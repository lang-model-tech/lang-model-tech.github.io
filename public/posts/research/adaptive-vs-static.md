# Evaluating adaptive paths vs static lesson plans
Benchmarks from our early E2-volve experiments on retention and completion.

## Study setup
We compared adaptive paths to fixed lesson plans across 3 campuses and 240 learners.

## Key findings
Adaptive paths improved completion by 11% and reduced time-to-mastery by 9 minutes per module.

## Implications
Small, targeted nudges outperform broad reminders. Tutor dashboards must stay concise to be actionable.
